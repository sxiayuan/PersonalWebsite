# Stephanie Xia — Interactive 3D Personal Website Context Document

---

# CORE PURPOSE

This website is not just a portfolio.

It is:

* a personal narrative,
* an engineering showcase,
* a creative experience,
* a technical demonstration,
* and a recruiter-facing identity platform.

The website should communicate:

* technical ability,
* creativity,
* systems thinking,
* startup-oriented mindset,
* storytelling ability,
* and multidisciplinary identity.

The goal is for recruiters, founders, engineers, designers, and collaborators to leave the site feeling:

> “This person is both technically capable and creatively driven. She builds things that feel intentional.”

---

# WEBSITE THEME

## Main Concept

The website explores growth through the lens of summers.

Each summer represents:

* experimentation,
* identity formation,
* building,
* exploration,
* ambition,
* and transition.

Instead of functioning like a traditional resume website, the experience should feel like:

> a cinematic journey through memories, projects, and evolving identity.

The emotional atmosphere should feel:

* warm,
* reflective,
* exploratory,
* ambitious,
* and slightly futuristic.

# DESIGN PRINCIPLES

## The website should feel:

### Intentional

Every animation and interaction should feel purposeful.

### Immersive

The user should feel like they are exploring a world rather than scrolling through a resume.

### Elegant

Avoid excessive visual noise.

### Human

The website should feel personal and emotional, not robotic.

### Technical

The engineering aspect should still remain visible through systems, architecture, and interaction design.

### Performant

Smoothness and responsiveness are more important than excessive effects.
